Frequently Asked Questions
When do I get approved? +
After you submit your application, our team reviews your details. Approval typically takes 1–3 business days. You will receive an email with instructions to access your reviewer dashboard.
How will I know I was accepted? +
You will receive a confirmation email at the address you registered with. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within 3 business days.
Is there any cost to join? +
No. Joining the Amazon Product Reviewer Program is completely free. There are no hidden fees or upfront costs.
How do I get paid? +
Earnings are paid out through your preferred payment method once you reach the minimum payout threshold. Track your earnings in your reviewer dashboard.
Do I need a large following? +
No specific follower count is required, but having an active presence on social media or a content platform helps your application.
